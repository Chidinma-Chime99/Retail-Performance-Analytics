# 📊 Retail Sales Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI sales analytics dashboard designed to provide a clear view of retail performance across revenue, growth, stores, categories, and time.

The dashboard transforms retail transaction data into actionable business insights, helping decision-makers quickly identify sales trends, high-performing stores, leading product categories, and changes in revenue performance.

📷 Dashboard Preview

![Retail Sales Performance Dashboard](Dashboard_Screenshot.png)

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to analyze retail sales performance and create an interactive dashboard that makes key business metrics easier to monitor and interpret.

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

- 💰 How much revenue is being generated?
- 📈 How is revenue changing month over month?
- 🏪 Which stores are generating the highest revenue?
- 🛒 Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
- 📅 How does revenue change across months?
- 📊 Which stores demonstrate the strongest profitability?

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI — Dashboard development and data visualization
- Power Query — Data transformation and preparation
- DAX — Measures and business calculations
- Data Modeling — Relationships between fact and dimension tables

📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard highlights several important performance indicators:

- Total Revenue: 180M
- Month-over-Month Growth: 19%
- Top Category: Grains

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of the business's current sales performance.

📈 Dashboard Highlights

Monthly Revenue Trend

The monthly revenue trend visual tracks changes in revenue over time, making it easier to identify periods of stronger or weaker sales performance.

Revenue increased from 26.7M in February to 33.8M in May, before declining to 28.2M in June.

🏪 Store Profitability

The store profitability analysis compares performance across six stores:

- Ikeja Superstore — 39M
- Lekki Mall Outlet — 36M
- Wuse Central — 34M
- Port Harcourt Waterfront — 26M
- Kano Sabon Gari — 23M
- Ibadan Ring Road — 22M

Ikeja Superstore recorded the highest profitability among the stores presented in the dashboard.

🛒 Revenue by Category

The category analysis shows the contribution of individual product categories to total revenue.

Grains is the leading category with 54M, followed by:

- Beverages — 26M
- Cooking Oil — 25M
- Dairy — 20M
- Pasta — 20M
- Canned Goods — 16M
- Noodles — 16M
- Household — 4M

This provides a clear view of the categories contributing most significantly to overall revenue.

💡 Business Insights

The analysis highlights several opportunities for business decision-making:

- Grains is the strongest revenue-generating category, making it an important category to monitor for inventory planning and sales strategy.
- Ikeja Superstore leads store profitability, suggesting an opportunity to examine the factors contributing to its stronger performance and potentially replicate successful practices across other locations.
- The 19% MoM growth indicates positive revenue momentum within the analyzed period.
- The monthly trend shows fluctuations in revenue, highlighting the importance of monitoring sales patterns and investigating the factors behind monthly increases and declines.
- Lower-performing categories and stores may require further investigation to identify opportunities for pricing, promotion, inventory, or operational improvements.
